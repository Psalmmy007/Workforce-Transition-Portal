StrataWork Global's AI Disruption Crisis

Following the enterprise-wide rollout of generative AI analytics agents, StrataWork automated 65% of entry-level and routine analytical workflows. While turnaround accelerated by 300%, hundreds of junior and mid-level analysts experienced sudden occupational redundancy with zero reskilling guidance.

Strategic Rationale of the Project
CareerGPS — Transforming Reactive Layoffs into Proactive Talent Transition
03 / 08

CareerGPS is an enterprise workforce intelligence platform built on 500 validated role records. It combines machine learning risk classification, academic benchmarking, and skills adjacency mapping into an interactive decision system.

Predict Displacement Trajectories

Developing empirical machine learning models quantifies role-level automation risks early, enabling leadership to anticipate workforce disruption instead of executing sudden reactionary layoffs.

Chart Reskilling Pathways

Equipping displaced personnel with automated skill adjacency mapping accelerates the transition of vulnerable professionals from routine executors into high-growth, strategic roles.

Preserve Human Capital Value

Harmonizing technological efficiency with proactive talent redeployment preserves institutional domain knowledge and fosters long-term workplace sustainability in the AI era.

Technical Architecture
End-to-End Predictive Pipeline From Raw Ingestion to Production Deployment
04 / 08
01. Ingest
500 enterprise records encompassing titles, skills index, AI adoption & risk classifications.
02. Clean
Missing value imputation, categorical encoding, feature scaling & multicollinearity audits.
03. Model
Multi-class Logistic Regression with 5-fold cross-validation benchmarked vs baseline.
04. Match
Skills gap vectorization mapping at-risk roles to 3 viable adjacent transition paths.
05. Deploy
Interactive multi-view Streamlit portal with real-time scoring and executive charts.
Training Dataset Specifications

500 validated enterprise role records spanning multi-industry domains. Features capture structural task automation probability, AI adoption rates, skill versatility indices, and compensation tiers.

Academic Benchmark Validation

Model classifications are empirically cross-referenced against the Oxford University Frey & Osborne (2013) Occupational Automation Index, ensuring credible academic grounding.

Product Features
Three Integrated Views Delivering Actionable Intelligence Across the Enterprise
05 / 08

Real-Time Risk Scoring

Users input their current job title, domain, industry, and skills index to receive instantaneous High, Medium, or Low automation susceptibility predictions powered by the trained ML pipeline.

Skills Adjacency Pathway Engine

For every vulnerable role, the platform computes skill overlap metrics and surfaces 3 tailored transition paths with specific skill-gap priorities and estimated transition feasibility.

Talent Analytics Executive Dashboard

Interactive Plotly visualizations and high-level KPI cards giving HR directors and chief people officers full portfolio visibility into departmental automation exposure.

Audited Methodology & Governance View

Complete transparency disclosing 5-fold CV accuracy, majority-class baseline lift, correlation analyses, and honest limitations documentation to establish corporate trust.

Engineered with branded enterprise aesthetics: circular favicon integration, dark/light theme persistence, dynamic calibration spinners, and automatic scroll-to-results UX flow.
